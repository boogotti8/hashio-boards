Method & honesty. This is a public-data RECONSTRUCTION of MIT's lead-indicator framework, not GMI/Real Vision's proprietary read. MIT publishes no numbered top-five list; the five reads below are the leads the MIT corpus names with a ~6-month horizon. Substitutions: GMI Financial Conditions Index → Chicago Fed NFCI (inverted); GMI Total Liquidity / Global M2 → US net liquidity (Fed BS − TGA − RRP); Regional Fed Survey Average 6-mo New Orders → z-composite of 3 FRED districts (Empire, Philadelphia, Dallas); the ISM new-orders sub-index is not free, so the headline ISM is shown as the T=0 clock. Lead times are MIT's own: FCI ~6–10m, liquidity ~6m (risk assets ~12wk), OECD CLI breadth ~6m, regional Fed 6-mo outlook = 6m. Impulse = 3-observation change ÷ its own sd, clipped ±1.5 (ISM adds a level term). Source: FRED, OECD SDMX, TradingEconomics, Binance, weather-for-risk. Corpus basis: MIT Business Cycle Updates Dec 2023–Jun 2026 (≈70 editions, gbrain mit-reports/).
