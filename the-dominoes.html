How every number on this board is built

  1. ISM Manufacturing PMI: TradingEconomics latest print, chained onto a 13-month ForexFactory backfill (cycle-falls-tracker/ism_history.csv). Each print is filed under the month TradingEconomics NAMES, and its own previous value under the month before it, so a revised or repeated print still lands on the right row.
  2. ISM New Orders: TradingEconomics — the leading sub-index that turns before headline PMI.
  3. Financial Conditions (FCI ex-oil): percent-ranked FRED basket DGS10 0.40 / DGS2 0.10 / DTWEXBGS 0.50 against a trailing 1000-day window, mapped 50 − (p − 0.5)·k so 50 = neutral and higher = looser. The scale slope k is fitted once on GMI's published anchors (50.48 on 2026-08-11 and 56.28 on 2026-02-11) and frozen, so the level is comparable to the reference and the history does not rescale as the calendar rolls; the basket rebuild runs each tick.
  4. Red trigger: house rule — a 5-point drawdown of the FCI ex-oil index from its trailing 24-month monthly peak.
  5. Lead window: FCI peak month + 9 months through today + 9 months (FCI leads the cycle ~9m).
  6. Global Liquidity YoY: broad money of US (M2SL) + China (PBoC M2) + euro area (ECB M3) + UK (BoE M4), each grown in local currency and weighted by its USD-converted stock. Local-currency growth avoids double-counting a FX revaluation as liquidity creation. Coverage: Japan omitted (no free keyless monthly feed) — ~97% of the basket's stock. GMI's 6.35% reading is a composite whose construction they do not publish; this is a proxy.
  7. US Total Liquidity (broad): Fed total assets (WALCL) + US M2 (M2SL), year-on-year %. This construction reproduced the reference's 4.29 on 2026-08-11 exactly.
  8. China Impulse: PBoC balance sheet, year-on-year % (liquidity_tracker.fetch_pboc).
  9. Cycle confirmations: four named rules — ISM above 50; ISM up over 3 months; New Orders above 50; global liquidity positive YoY. The chip counts how many currently pass.
  10. PUBLIC-DATA RECONSTRUCTION of the reference board's STRUCTURE, not GMI's proprietary read. Their unpublished inputs (Global Liquidity composite, house FCI) are proxied and labelled.
