HOW EVERY NUMBER ON THIS BOARD IS BUILT
30d vol = annualised sd of the last 30 daily log returns x sqrt(252); for yields, the annualised sd of daily yield CHANGES divided by the current yield (relative yield vol).
Risk = NORMAL < 20% <= RISKY < 40% <= HIGH RISK.
Secular trend = sign of the OLS slope of log price over the last 200 sessions (~40 weeks). Weekly trend = sign of the OLS slope of log price over the last 50 sessions (~10 weeks).
Signal change = NEW BUY / NEW SELL when the weekly-trend slope flips sign within the last 30 sessions (~6 weeks); SINCE = the date of that flip.
Liquidity = GMI-style Total Global Liquidity rebuilt from the G5 central banks (Fed WALCL, ECB ECBASSETSW, BOJ JPNASSETS, PBoC total assets, BoE consolidated balance sheet) converted to USD at the Fed H.10 rate for the SAME date; YoY vs 364d ago, 6m/6m vs 183d ago; direction = rising if YoY > +1% and 6m/6m > 0, falling if both are negative, else flat.
Business cycle = ISM manufacturing PMI: TradingEconomics live print merged into the stored monthly history; the business-cycle trend is rising while ISM >= 50.
Regime matrix = liquidity×economy → rising/rising = Full Cycle Expansion · flat/rising = Late-Cycle Expansion · falling/rising = Late-Cycle Squeeze · rising/falling = Liquidity-Led Recovery · flat/falling = Rolling Over · falling/falling = Full Cycle Contraction.
DXY = level = live ICE quote (CNBC); history = DXY rebuilt with the official ICE formula from the six Fed H.10 component pairs (DEXUSEU, DEXJPUS, DEXUSUK, DEXCAUS, DEXSDUS, DEXSZUS).
Proxies = XAU: 30d vol / trends measured on GLD (SPDR Gold Shares); level is COMEX gold front month; HG: 30d vol / trends measured on CPER (United States Copper Index Fund); level is COMEX copper front month; NDX: FRED NASDAQ100 cash index (no fund proxy needed); CL: level is the WTI front month (CNBC); vol/trends on FRED WTI spot (DCOILWTICO).
