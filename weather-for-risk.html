HOW THE TWO GAUGES ARE BUILT — AND WHY THE FUEL GAUGE LEADS PRICES
Growth = 5.0 × (ISM − 50) + 1.5 × (3m ISM change).
Liquidity = 10 × (US liquidity YoY − 4%) + 6 × (G5 CB YoY − 2%) — i.e. measured against a neutral money-growth rate.
Conditions = 2.0 × (index − 50) + 7.5 × (6m index change), where the index is our GMI-style FCI ex-oil (0.50 broad dollar · 0.40 10y · 0.10 2y, each rank-scored over a trailing ~4y), rebased so 50 = neutral and higher = looser. Cycle-top trigger = the monthly index sitting ≥ 4pts below its loosest monthly reading of the past 24 months.
Dollar = −10 × (DXY 1m %) − 4 × (DXY 3m %).
Cycle gauge (economy now) = 0.55·growth + 0.25·liquidity + 0.20·conditions.
Markets’ fuel (leads BTC ~6m, NDX ~1–2m) = 0.35·liquidity + 0.35·conditions + 0.30·dollar.

What each input means
LIQUIDITY — THE FUEL
How much money is in the system. More money → higher asset prices. We use the US-native measure so a strong dollar can’t fake a tightening: Fed balance sheet + US M2, with the G5 central banks as the global leg.
BUSINESS CYCLE — THE ENGINE
Is the economy growing? ISM above 50 and rising = yes. In expansions, dips in healthy assets get bought.
CONDITIONS — THE EARLY WARNING
Dollar + rates (oil stripped out), our reconstruction of GMI’s FCI ex-oil. It turns ~9 months before the economy does (the validated lead). Right now: RED — tightened 31.1pts off its loosest point of the past 24m.
DOLLAR — THE MODIFIER
A falling dollar is fuel for crypto and high-beta risk. A bouncing dollar makes global money look weaker than it is — an optical effect we correct for by keeping the liquidity leg US-native.
THE VERDICT — THE OUTPUT
The four inputs resolve to one instruction, recomputed live: reduce risk → hold → buy selectively → buy the dips. It can never contradict the gauges, because it is the gauges.
