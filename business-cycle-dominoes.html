How every number is built
PositionsMonths relative to the ISM (month 0) are the reference chart's own: China Credit Impulse +16, GMI FCI +9, New Orders minus Inventories +3, New Orders +1.5, O/T Hours Worked -1.5, Durable Goods -2.5, Industrial Production -4, Cyclical Jobs -5, Earnings -5, Unemployment -6, CPI -7, Wages -13, CPI Shelter -16. Right of 0 leads the cycle; left of 0 lags it.
DirectionThe sign of the metric's 3-month change, with a dead band of a quarter of that series' own typical 3-month move over the last 120 observations. A move inside the band reads FLAT rather than as a turn.
TurnedThe most recent month in which the 3-month change changed sign and cleared the dead band -- i.e. the last time that domino actually toppled.
GMI Financial Conditions IndexPercent-ranked FRED basket (DGS10 0.40 / DGS2 0.10 / DTWEXBGS 0.50) over a trailing 1000-day window, mapped 50 - (p-0.5)*100, slope calibrated to GMI's own published anchors. 50 = neutral, higher = looser. Read daily; the ladder position is the month-end.
China Credit ImpulsePBoC total assets, % change on a year ago. GMI's impulse is a broader credit aggregate; this is the documented public proxy.
Cyclical JobsConstruction (FRED USCONS) plus transportation & warehousing (CES4300000001) payrolls, summed, % change on a year ago.
EarningsS&P 500 trailing twelve-month EPS from multpl.com, % change on a year ago.
ISM New Orders / InventoriesTradingEconomics prints. No free public history feed exists for the two sub-indices, so the monthly print is accumulated locally; the direction reads off the 1-month change until four prints are held.
Every number is pulled from free public data and recomputed on this box. A public-data RECONSTRUCTION of the reference chart's structure and its lead/lag ladder — not GMI's proprietary read, and not investment advice.
