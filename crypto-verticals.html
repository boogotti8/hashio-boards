HOW THIS IS BUILT — PUBLIC DATA ONLY
basket = equal-weighted, daily-rebalanced index of its live Binance spot members (the mean of the members' daily returns, base 100); a member needs 60+ daily closes, and a basket needs two live members and 200+ sessions of its own history before a 200-day line exists. today = last close vs the prior UTC daily close. mtd = last close vs the last close of the previous calendar month. above 50D/200D = basket level vs its own 50/200-session SMA. 50D rising = SMA50 today is higher than it was 10 sessions ago; 200D rising = SMA200 today is higher than it was 20 sessions ago (the LINE is up, not the price). rows are ranked by MTD. CORE = the vertical's largest, most liquid Binance-listed names; FRONTIER = its smaller, higher-beta listed names. The split is ours, not the vendor's.

sources: Binance spot daily klines (api.binance.com), no key.
This board is a public-data RECONSTRUCTION of the verticals table — the baskets, weights and thresholds are ours, and are not the vendor's proprietary sector indices or their signal engine.
