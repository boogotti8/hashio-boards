How this board is built

Monitor leg. The Monitor leg is the Alpha Risk Monitor's own engine: a 50-session OLS slope of log price (~10 weeks) with yields fitted on the level, and SINCE = the date that slope last changed sign. Imported from /home/dietpi/alpha-risk-monitor/build.py so the two boards cannot disagree.
Regime leg. The Regime leg scores three live impulses: growth G from ISM (level vs 50 and the 3-month change), liquidity L from the GMI-style Total Global Liquidity Index (USD-stock-weighted broad money for US+CN+EA+UK, YoY level and 3-month change), and rates R = +1 when the 10-year weekly trend is falling, -1 when rising.
Score. score = w_growth*G + w_liquidity*L + w_rates*R per asset; |score| >= 0.45 gives a directional regime call, otherwise MIXED. The weights are the reconstruction's assumed regime exposures (registry.json) — a judgment panel. The inputs are live, so the calls move with the data.
Agreement. ALIGNED = both legs point the same way for the asset (for bonds, price direction); DIVERGING = they do not, including where one leg is MIXED.
Equities. Equities: the level is the S&P 500 cash index (FRED SP500) and the trend is measured on it; the Nasdaq 100 trend from the Monitor board is shown beside it.
Crest base case. The crest base case is the midpoint of the domino lead window: [FCI six-month peak + 9 months] → [last official monthly read + 9 months]. The forwarded snapshot's own base case (~2027-03) sits inside that window.
Live inputs. GMI Total Global Liquidity +6.18% YoY (3m -0.20pt, 1m -0.14pt, to 2026-08-01, $100.7tn over US+CN+EA+UK) ISM 54.6 (3m +0.6pt, 2026-08-01).
